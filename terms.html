1. What Twin Poster is

Twin Poster ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It is operated by its developer for his own use: it uploads videos created by the developer to TikTok and Instagram accounts that he owns or manages.

2. Who may use it

The App is not distributed to the public. Only the developer and the accounts he connects himself use it. Each account is connected through the official TikTok Login Kit or Meta login, and access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Instagram app settings.

3. Content

Only original content made by the developer, or content he has the rights to, is uploaded. The account owner reviews every post. With TikTok, videos are sent as drafts to the TikTok inbox, or posted directly only after the owner confirms. All content must follow the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service and the Instagram Terms of Use.

4. Third-party platforms

The App uses the official TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram Graph API. Their own terms also apply. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Meta.
