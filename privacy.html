Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Summary

Twin Poster is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no other users.

2. Data the App receives

  • From TikTok (Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar. These are used only to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth tokens (TikTok access and refresh tokens, Meta access token). These are used only to upload videos to the connected accounts.
  • Video files and captions, created by the owner and uploaded through the TikTok Content Posting API (scopes video.upload and video.publish) and the Instagram Graph API.

3. Storage

All data is stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. It is never sent anywhere except to TikTok and Meta, through their official APIs, to perform the upload.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Deleting the local file removes all stored data.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

7. Contact

For questions, open an issue at github.com/Unisell-v0/twin-poster/issues.
